Rudder

Decide with real confidence, not endless research or gut-feel.

Rudder is an iPhone app that turns a hard decision — a purchase, a subscription, a trip, anything you've been going in circles on — into a clear, well-reasoned recommendation.

How it's different

  • It asks only what it can't work out itself. One question at a time, never a generic form.
  • It researches what it can verify instead of guessing, and never invents a source it didn't actually retrieve.
  • It attacks its own answer before showing it to you — the recommendation only stands if it beats the strongest alternative across realistic variations in what matters to you.
  • It shows you what could make it wrong, so you can judge for yourself instead of taking its word for it.

Built on Claude

Rudder's reasoning, research and self-challenge pipeline runs on Anthropic's Claude, including Claude's own web search tool for the research step. It is a small, solo-built product, currently finishing App Store preparation.

Common questions

How do I get more deep decisions?

Your first deep decision is always free. After that, Rudder Pro removes the limit — start a free trial or subscribe from Profile ▸ Your plan.

I subscribed but Rudder still shows Free.

Open Profile and tap "Restore Purchases." If that doesn't resolve it, check that the subscription is active under Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ Subscriptions on your iPhone, and that you're signed in with the same Apple Account used to purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Rudder cannot cancel a subscription on your behalf — manage or cancel it directly in your Apple Account: Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ Subscriptions ▸ Rudder Pro ▸ Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Profile ▸ Your data in the app to delete your decisions, Decision Memory, or outcomes individually or all at once. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details on what is and isn't stored.

Is Rudder's advice reliable?

Rudder uses an AI model and can be wrong. It shows its assumptions and what it couldn't verify so you can judge for yourself, and it is not financial, legal, medical, or other professional advice.

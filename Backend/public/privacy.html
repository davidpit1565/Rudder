Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Rudder ("the app") is a decision-assistance app. This policy describes exactly what leaves your device, why, and what stays on it.

What is sent, and why

When you ask Rudder to analyse a decision, the text of that decision, any answers you give to its follow-up questions, and any preferences you've previously approved storing are sent to Rudder's own backend service, which forwards them to a third-party AI provider, Anthropic, to produce the analysis. This is the only reason any of this content leaves your device — Rudder cannot answer without it.

No account is required to use Rudder, and none of this content is linked to your identity: no name, email, phone number, physical address, or device identifier is attached to it.

Third-party processing (Anthropic)

Anthropic processes the decision content you send solely to generate the analysis returned to you. It is handled under Anthropic's own commercial API terms and privacy policy (see anthropic.com/legal/privacy and anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms for their current terms) — as of this writing, Anthropic does not use data submitted through its API to train its models by default. Confirm the terms in effect at anthropic.com before relying on this statement, since a provider's policy can change independently of this page.

What stays on your device

Your decisions, any preferences Rudder has learned and you've approved ("Decision Memory"), and any outcomes you record are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Rudder's backend does not keep a copy of your decision text after returning the analysis — it processes each request and discards it.

Deleting your data

You control this data completely:

  • Delete a single decision, all decisions, all Decision Memory, or all recorded outcomes from Profile ▸ Your data in the app.
  • Deleting the app from your device removes everything stored locally.

No accounts, no tracking, no advertising

Rudder has no sign-up, no user accounts, and does not track you across apps or websites. There is no advertising identifier, no analytics SDK, and no ad network integrated into the app.

Subscription status

Whether you have an active Rudder Pro subscription is read directly from Apple's StoreKit on your device to unlock Pro features. Rudder's app does not transmit your subscription status anywhere itself. Apple's own handling of your purchase is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not this one.

Children

Rudder is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone. It carries a 4+ age rating on the App Store.

Changes to this policy

If what Rudder collects or how it's processed changes, this page will be updated before that change ships, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect it.

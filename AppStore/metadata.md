## Description (4,000 characters)

```
RUDDER helps you make one decision well.

Tell it what you're trying to figure out, in your own words. It works out what
the decision actually turns on, looks up what it can find, and asks you only the
things it genuinely cannot know. Often that's nothing at all.

Then it gives you an answer you can act on: which option fits you, why, what
you're giving up by taking it, and how stable that recommendation is.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

It does the work, so you don't fill in a form.
Most decision tools start by interrogating you. RUDDER starts by analysing. A
question only reaches you when the answer could genuinely change the
recommendation and there's no way for RUDDER to find it out itself.

It tells you how strong the recommendation is.
Every recommendation is re-run under different priorities and assumptions. If the
same option stays ahead, it's Strong. If reasonable changes would flip it, it's
Moderate. If nothing stays ahead, RUDDER says there isn't a clear winner rather
than inventing one.

It argues against itself.
Before showing you anything, RUDDER looks for the strongest case against its own
recommendation — a missing fact, a bad assumption, an option it underrated. If
that case wins, it changes its mind and tells you.

It shows you the trade-off.
There's almost always something you give up. RUDDER names it instead of selling
you the upside.

You decide.
RUDDER never asks you to accept its answer. Choose something else and it won't
argue, won't ask if you're sure, and won't try to change your mind — it'll show
you what you're gaining and what you're giving up, and save your choice.

WHAT YOU CAN USE IT FOR

- Which laptop, phone or camera to buy
- Whether to take a job offer
- Which apartment to choose
- Whether a subscription is still worth it
- Which of two trips to book
- Anything where you've been going round in circles

YOUR DECISIONS STAY YOURS

Decisions, learned preferences and outcomes are stored on your device and kept
separately, so you can delete any of them on their own — or all of it at once.
There's no account to create, and no sign-up before your first decision.

RUDDER PRO

Every decision gets the same analysis, free or paid: the same research, the same
stress testing, the same self-challenge. Pro removes the limits.

Your first deep decision is always free, and your last ten decisions stay in
view without Pro. A free trial lets you try Pro itself before paying. Pro makes
deep decisions and history unlimited, and adds Decision Memory — which learns
what you actually care about from decisions you have already made, stores only
what you approve, and takes account of how those decisions turned out.

Pro is a monthly or annual subscription. Payment is charged to your Apple Account
at confirmation. It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours
before the end of the period; manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings.

A NOTE ON WHAT THIS IS

RUDDER uses an AI model to analyse your decision. It can be wrong. It shows you
what it assumed, what it couldn't verify, and what would change its answer, so
you can judge for yourself. It is not financial, legal, medical or professional
advice.
```

## Age rating

4+ — no objectionable content. Answer Apple's AI questionnaire honestly: the app
sends the text of a decision to a service that uses a third-party AI model, and
model output is shown to the user. It has no user-generated content shared
between users and no moderation surface.

## Support and marketing URLs

Set in `Config/Shared.xcconfig`, and empty until you publish the pages. The app
treats a missing or non-HTTPS URL as absent and hides the link, so nothing ships
pointing at a page that does not exist.
